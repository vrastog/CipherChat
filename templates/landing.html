How It Works

Three steps to a secure session

The entire handshake completes in milliseconds before your first message is sent.

1

Connect & Authenticate

Log in with your credentials. Your session is established securely before any crypto handshake begins.

2

ECDH Handshake

Both browsers generate ephemeral key pairs and exchange public keys via the relay server. The shared AES key is derived independently — never transmitted.

3

Encrypted Chat

Every message is encrypted, signed, and sent as a binary blob. The server sees only ciphertext — decryption happens only in your browser.
